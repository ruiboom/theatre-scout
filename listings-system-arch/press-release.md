# Press release — draft

> Drafted for 1 September 2026 launch. **Strategic note:** this press moment should likely be **deferred** to the MCP/AI launch in Oct/Nov, where the story is much stronger. Keep this draft on hand either way.

---

**FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE**

**London gets a guide to its other theatre scene**

LONDON, 1 September 2026 — A new website launching today aims to make London's overlooked theatre scene easier to navigate.

*Anywhere But The West End* is a free listings platform covering 70 non-West End venues across the capital — pub theatres, fringe spaces, and converted warehouses where much of London's most ambitious new work appears. Tickets at these venues typically run £10–£25, compared with £80 or more for major West End productions.

"There are two theatre scenes in London," says founder [Name]. "One has billboards. The other is where the actually interesting work happens — and it's a fraction of the price. I built the site I wanted to use."

The platform aggregates listings from venues including [3–4 of the best-known you cover — e.g. Bush Theatre, Park Theatre, Soho Theatre, Battersea Arts Centre]. New listings are added weekly. A free email newsletter highlights upcoming shows.

*Anywhere But The West End* is independently run and not affiliated with any venue or producer.

**Press contact:** [Name, email]

---

## Strategic notes

- A pure listings-site launch is a **weak press hook**. Journalists need a *reason for it to exist now*.
- The strong press hook is the AI/MCP angle: *"Solo dev builds AI-native discovery for London's overlooked theatre scene."* That story re-pitches all the same theatre partners, lands better with tech press, and gives you something genuinely novel to point at.
- **Recommendation:** soft launch on 1 Sept (no press), build the MCP server through autumn, run press in Oct/Nov with the AI angle as the headline.
- If you do launch press in September anyway, this draft works — but expect modest pickup.
